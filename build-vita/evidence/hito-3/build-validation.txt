Hardware finding: the first normal-probe attempt (before the fix below) crashed natively on every
one of three launches, with no boot.log record beyond "PASS logger" - confirmed as three
psp2core-*.psp2dmp crash dumps rather than a controlled failure. VitaSDK's newlib heap defaults to
128 MiB; Memory::MemorySystem alone requests ~138.5 MiB (128 MiB FCRAM + 6 MiB VRAM + 4 MiB N3DS
extra RAM + 0.5 MiB DSP RAM) via plain heap allocations, plus a ~5 MiB per-process page table, which
exceeds that default. Fixed by defining `_newlib_heap_size_user = 192 MiB` in system_main.cpp (the
documented VitaSDK mechanism for this, from share/gcc-arm-vita-eabi/samples/newlib_heapsize_ctrl -
not the "system mode" MEMSIZE budget vita-make-fself also exposes, which is a different, unrelated
knob). The VPK above already includes this fix; the SHA-256 differs from the one first installed.
